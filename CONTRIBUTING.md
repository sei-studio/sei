# Contributing to Sei

Thanks for helping out. Here is how it works.

## Ideas and feature requests

Open an [issue](https://github.com/sei-studio/sei/issues) and describe what you want in plain words: what you were doing, and what you wish Sei did. You don't need a design or a spec. A couple of sentences is plenty. We read every one and build the ones that fit.

## Bug reports

Open an [issue](https://github.com/sei-studio/sei/issues) and include:

- What happened, and what you expected instead
- Steps to make it happen again, if you know them
- Your Sei version (Settings), your OS, and the game and game version
- Which AI you use: Sei Cloud, your ChatGPT plan, your own key, or Ollama
- A screenshot or short clip if it shows the problem

## Pull requests

- Target the `dev` branch.
- Keep each PR focused on one change. Small PRs get reviewed faster.
- Run `npm run typecheck` and `npm test` before you open it, and fix anything you broke.
- Say what you changed and how you tested it.

For anything big, open an issue or ask on Discord first so we can agree on the approach. [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) has the architecture notes and the rules the codebase follows, and the README shows how to [run it from source](README.md#development).
