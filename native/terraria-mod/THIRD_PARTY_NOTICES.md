# Third-party notices for the Sei companion mod (Terraria)

The mod under `SeiCompanion/` is MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`). No code from
other mods is included. These projects were read as references for how a
companion can live in Terraria; nothing was copied from them:

- TerraGuardians (companion NPCs that fight and follow): design reference only.
- AICompanion: design reference only.
- TerraBlind: its repository carries no license file, so it is treated as all
  rights reserved and was used as a reference for ideas only.

The mod draws the companion with Terraria's own player renderer and the
vanilla Guide sprite as a fallback. It ships no art of its own.

tModLoader (MIT) and Terraria are not redistributed with the mod; the mod is
compiled against the player's own tModLoader install.
